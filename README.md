In the first phase we had a unique dataset with the measurement of five metabolites from seven regions of the brain. The challenge was to select the right method to analyse the data and gain meaningful insights on the relationships among the metabolites within and across the different brain regions. In this phase I had made an explorative analyses through clustering techniques (KNN, Hierarchical clustering, PCA). Although some interesting observations were made, a more meaningful and intuitive approach was achieved through network analyses (w/graph theory). This novel approach provided very interesting insights over the physiology of the neurometabolites (Rodriguez Nieto et al. 2023; https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S105381192200951X).  

In the second phase, we aimed to investigate whether the neurochemical networks were affected by diabetes. These scripts were used to generate graph theory analyses (GTA) metrics and figures.
